<ProblemSection

      The Problem:
      <br />
      Power users were leaving Trailhead for ChatGPT, YouTube, and Slack communities the moment they hit a real Salesforce problem at work.
    </>
  }
>

Salesforce Trailhead was built for the climb - learning, upskilling & getting certified.<br /><br />But once users become more competent and are in work environments, their needs shifted from learning paths to real problems at work.

<InlineMetrics
  metrics={[
    { value: "6 of 8", label: "power users preferred Solve over ChatGPT for Salesforce questions" },
    { value: "64%", label: "disengaged after hitting major milestones" },
    { value: "36%", label: "found recommendations not adaptive enough" },
  ]}
/>

So they left for ChatGPT, YouTube, and Slack communities, where their needs were met.<br /><br />Aside from coming back a few times a year to get their certifications renewed, the platform provided them with nothing of practical, immediate value.<br /><br />Re-engagement was the actual problem, not retention.

</ProblemSection>

<SectionDivider />

<SolutionSection
  title={
    <>
      The Solution:
      <br />
      We built two layers, working together.
    </>
  }
>

Trailhead Solve intercepts power users at the moment they'd otherwise open ChatGPT or Slack communities.<br /><br />We built a RAG-powered assistant that takes a real Salesforce problem in plain language and returns a sourced answer linked back to Trailhead modules and docs.

<Figure
  videoSrc="/case-study-assets/solve-answering.mp4"
  caption="Solve takes a question in plain language and returns a sourced answer."
/>

The redesigned dashboard picks up where Solve leaves off, surfacing recommendations shaped by each user's behavior on the platform.<br /><br />It learns from each user's Solve conversations to recommend what they should learn next, based on the gaps their own questions reveal.

<Figure
  src="/case-study-assets/solve-learningrecommendations.svg"
  caption="Recommends what to learn next, based on the gaps each user's Solve questions reveal."
/>

It also surfaces community questions they're uniquely qualified to answer, giving power users a reason to engage inside Trailhead as contributors, not just learners.

<Figure
  src="/case-study-assets/solve-communityquestions.svg"
  caption="Surfaces community questions they're uniquely qualified to answer based on a user's profile."
/>

It also addresses something power users said in nearly every interview:<br /><br />*"Trailhead's learning content is just too basic for where I'm at in my career."*<br /><br />We introduced AI-generated learning paths shaped by where the user actually is and what jobs at their level are hiring for.

<Figure
  videoSrc="/case-study-assets/solve-customtrailmix.mp4"
  caption="Learning paths shaped by where the user is and what jobs at their level are hiring for."
/>

</SolutionSection>
