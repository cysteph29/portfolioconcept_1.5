<ProblemSection

      The Problem:
      <br />
      Power Users of Salesforce Trailhead were disengaging with the platform.
    </>
  }
>

Salesforce Trailhead was built for the climb - learning, up skilling & getting certified.<br /><br />But once users became more competent and started solving real problems at work, their needs shifted from learning paths to real problems at work.

<InlineMetrics
  metrics={[

/>

So they left for ChatGPT, YouTube, and Slack communities, where their needs were met.<br /><br />Aside from coming back a few times a year to get their certifications renewed, the platform provided them with nothing of practical, immediate value.<br /><br />Re-engagement was the actual problem, not retention.

</ProblemSection>

<SectionDivider />

<SolutionSection
  title={
    <>
      The Solution:
      <br />
      We built two layers, working together — Trailhead Solve, and a Redesigned Dashboard.
    </>
  }
>

Trailhead Solve intercepts power users at the moment they'd otherwise open ChatGPT or Slack communities.

<Figure caption="Prototype v1 of embedded assistant with contextual prompts." />

A RAG-powered assistant that takes a real Salesforce problem in plain language and returns a sourced answer linked back to Trailhead modules and docs.

<Figure caption="Prototype v2 of recommendation and next-step behavior." />

<PullQuote>
  If the user is still stuck, Solve drafts a Trailblazer Community post for them, pre-filled and ready to publish.
</PullQuote>

<Figure caption="Prototype v3 detailing in-flow progression guidance." />

The redesigned dashboard picks up where Solve leaves off. It reads each user's absence pattern to surface the one thing most likely to pull them back.<br /><br />It surfaces certification maintenance deadlines, learning recommendations based on conversations with Solve, and community questions they're uniquely qualified to answer.
