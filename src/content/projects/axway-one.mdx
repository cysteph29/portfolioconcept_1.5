      <br />

Axway&apos;s E-Invoicing platform keeps enterprises compliant with e-invoicing regulations across Europe.<br /><br />Behind it sits an operations team that fixes failed invoices all day.<br /><br />Every day, hundreds of invoices fail compliance checks, and before the team can fix one, they have to figure out two things first.<br /><br />Which invoice failed, and why it failed.

<Figure
  src="/problem.png"
  alt="Legacy invoice triage spread across disconnected tools."
  caption="Current-state audit of the fragmented invoice exception workflow."
/>

Most of these screens were built for developers a decade earlier. Operations would jump between them to piece together what went wrong. The information was all there, just not built for the people doing the work.<br /><br />The cost wasn&apos;t just time. Operations doubles as enterprise support.<br /><br />When accounting professionals at customer companies couldn&apos;t figure out why their invoice failed, they&apos;d call operations. Every slow diagnosis meant a delayed fix, a missed SLA, and another inbound ticket.

</ProblemSection>

<SectionDivider />

<SolutionSection
  title={
    <>
      The Solution:
      <br />
      Two changes to the invoice list, surfacing what users were already hunting for.
    </>
  }
>

The diagnosis flow didn&apos;t need new screens. It needed the existing data to show up where users were already looking.

<Figure
  src="/solution.png"
  alt="Axway One invoice exception dashboard with prioritized queue."
  caption="A unified queue that ranks exceptions by business impact and SLA risk."
/>

We added a Status column to the main invoice list, so failed invoices were visible at a glance instead of buried in a sub-task screen.<br /><br />We also added a Details button next to each invoice, putting the &ldquo;why it failed&rdquo; answer one click away instead of four.

<Figure
  src="/solution2.png"
  alt="Structured issue intake with required evidence fields."
  caption="Structured intake reduced incomplete submissions and back-and-forth."
/>
